## 5. Detailed Ingredients & Material Specifications

### A. Femi9 Organic Sanitary Pads (9-Layer Material Breakdown)

1. **Top Sheet:** 100% Certified Organic Cotton (Unbleached, hypoallergenic, skin-friendly).
2. **Side Leakage Guard:** Hydrophobic non-woven plant-fiber raised borders.
3. **Active Anion Comfort Strip:** Plant-extracted negative ion strip with chitin & far-infrared minerals (for anti-bacterial protection, odor neutralization, and cramp soothing).
4. **Flow Distribution Layer:** Soft cotton fiber matrix for rapid fluid spreading without pooling.
5. **Upper Air-Laid Paper:** High-porosity dust-free paper wrapping for directional moisture transport.
6. **Absorbent SAP Core:** Plant-derived Super Absorbent Polymer (SAP) gel core that locks liquid into dry gel.
7. **Lower Air-Laid Paper:** Structural paper wrapper maintaining flat pad geometry.
8. **Ultra-Thin Cotton Base Layer:** Cushioning layer for flexibility without stiffness.
9. **Breathable Back Sheet:** Biodegradable micro-porous polyethylene film for 100% airflow with zero leakage.
10. **Adhesive & Release Paper:** Non-toxic food-grade pressure-sensitive adhesive.

> **Zero-Nasty Guarantee:** 100% free from elemental chlorine bleach, synthetic dyes, artificial fragrances, pesticides, dioxins, and phthalates.
